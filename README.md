# Business Analyst Case Study: Healthcare Requirements Gathering & Documentation

## Executive Overview
This portfolio project demonstrates end-to-end Business Analysis and requirements engineering for an operational workflow redesign in a healthcare setting. The project addresses lobby congestion and high insurance claim rejection rates by replacing manual paper check-ins with an automated digital intake and real-time verification system.

---

## Key Business Outcomes Target
* **73% Reduction in Intake Cycle Time:** Streamlining check-in from 15 minutes down to under 4 minutes per patient.
* **30% Reduction in Claim Denials:** Preventing demographic and policy typos before claims reach insurance payers.
* **15 Hours/Week Saved:** Eliminating manual data re-keying and verification phone calls for front-desk staff.
